    About

    Fears of Love is a free ice-breaker game for two, built around moments from films, series and books.

    Developed by TomBlackroad. It is open source: see the code or suggest a scene.

    Scene descriptions are written in our own words; titles and artwork belong to their owners. Posters are served by TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

    This site counts visits anonymously (no cookies, no personal data) with GoatCounter.
