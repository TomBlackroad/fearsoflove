    About

    Fears of Love is a free ice-breaker game for two (or more), built around moments from movies, series and books.

    Developed by TomBlackroad. It is open source: see the code or suggest a scene.

    Scene descriptions are written in our own words; titles and artwork belong to their owners. Posters are served by TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

    Anonymous usage stats are on: we count visits, countries and taps on cards with PostHog (EU), without cookies, without storing anything on your device and without keeping your IP address. Your browser asks not to be tracked, so nothing is sent.

    Before you switch on dares

    • For a group in one room. Pass the phone around; each card is a dare or a truth for whoever holds it.
    • Spicy content. 🌶️ Flirty is playful (a cheek kiss, a compliment). 🌶️🌶️ Steamy is more intimate, best for couples or close friends. Nothing is explicit.
    • Anyone can pass, always. Choosing the truth instead of the dare is part of the game.
