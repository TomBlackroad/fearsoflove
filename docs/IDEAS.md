# Feature ideas

Grouped by what they would do for the project. Stars mark a quick win (★) or a big bet (◆).

## Make long distance feel like being together
- ★ **Reactions in rooms**: tap a heart, laugh or gasp and it floats up on everyone's screen. Uses the same peer-to-peer channel.
- ★ **Who answers first**: a random "you start" badge per card, rotating between players. Great for 3+ people.
- **Nicknames and colors** in rooms (stored only on the device), so the pill shows "Ana, Tom and Leo".
- **Voice or video inside the room**: the library already supports audio/video streams; a small "call" button would remove the need for another app.
- **Reliability**: add a TURN relay (Cloudflare, free up to 1,000 GB per month) for people on strict office or mobile networks.
- **Host controls**: lock the room, or let only the host move the deck.

## Make the game deeper
- ★ **Quick reactions per title** (loved it, seen it, want to see, not for us). In rooms this becomes "You agree on 7 of 9 titles", the seed of the recommender.
- **"Our watchlist"**: every title you both mark "want to see" is collected on one list you can share or copy.
- **Compatibility round**: 10 cards with two-option answers ("I'd leave" or "I'd stay"); the end screen shows where you match and the one card worth talking about.
- **Spice levels**: a flirty or 18+ deck behind an age check; a "family" deck for parents and kids.
- **Themed decks**: Halloween horror, Valentine's, Christmas films, summer romance, "first dates only".
- **Daily card**: the same card for everyone each day. A reason to come back, and good for social posts.

## Grow the audience
- ★ **Share a card as an image** (story format with the poster, the scene and the question). This is the cheapest growth channel.
- ★ **Spanish and French** versions (you already planned this). Translate the cards in data files, one language per file.
- **Install as an app and play offline** (a service worker on top of the existing app icon).
- **Community scenes**: a moderated "suggest a scene" form that opens a GitHub issue, with credit for accepted cards.
- **Events**: printed decks for bars, cinemas and bookstores ("Fears of Love night"). Speed-dating nights with the cards are the offline test of the dating idea.

## Toward a recommender or a dating product ◆
- **Scene-based profiles**: instead of photos first, people show three scenes they would talk about and how they answered.
- **Matching by stories**: overlap in loved titles plus answer style, explained to users ("You both cried at Up and would both stay in the Matrix").
- **Conversation starters** for new matches, built from shared titles. This reuses the cards directly.
- **Import Letterboxd or Goodreads** to build a personal deck from what you have actually seen and read.
- Plan for: accounts, an 18+ check, moderation, reporting and blocking, and legal review (see DATA_COLLECTION.md).

## Possible income later (if you want it)
- Print-on-demand physical card decks.
- Paid themed packs, while the base game stays free.
- Partnerships with cinemas, bookstores or streaming events.
